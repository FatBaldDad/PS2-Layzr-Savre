## No Guarantee of Protection

Layzr Savre is intended to study, monitor, and eventually help protect the PS2 optical-drive system.

At this stage, the project is experimental and should not be considered a guaranteed protection device.

No claim is made that this project will prevent all laser, servo, optical-drive, or console damage.

Protection behavior must be tested, validated, and documented before any public kit release.
